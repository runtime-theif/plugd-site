How it works

Three moves. That's it.

  1. Show the card

    Hold it up. Plugd spots the card, snaps the front, then asks for the back. No shutter button needed.

  2. Say a note

    "Wants a demo in March." Talk while it saves; your note is written down next to the contact.

  3. Send it on

    Tag hot, warm or cold, then export the whole event to Excel, a CSV for your CRM, or your phone's contacts.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Plugd free?

Yes. Plugd is free to download and use, with no scan limits and no subscription. Card reading uses an AI provider you choose with your own API key; Google Gemini and OpenRouter offer free tiers, and any usage charges are between you and that provider.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and scan. There is no Plugd account and nothing to log in to.

What is the best free business card scanner for trade shows?

If you exhibit at events, look for a scanner that handles paper cards, badge QR codes and NFC, lets you qualify leads on the spot and exports to your CRM. Plugd does all of that for free, with no account, and keeps every lead on your phone.

How does Plugd read the cards?

Plugd sends the card photos to an AI model from the provider you choose in Settings (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude or OpenRouter), using an API key you add. The model reads the name, title, company, phone, email, website, LinkedIn and address, and Plugd fills in the contact. Because it uses modern AI models, it reads cards in many languages and layouts.

Does it work with QR codes, event badges and NFC cards?

Yes. Switch to QR to scan a code on a conference badge or a digital business card, or to NFC to tap a card or tag to your phone (NFC needs a phone that supports it).

Can I scan business cards to Excel?

Yes. Export any event, or a filtered slice such as only hot leads, to an Excel .xlsx file with one row per contact, including your tag and voice note.

Can I get my leads into my CRM?

Export an event as a CSV and import it into HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive or any CRM that accepts spreadsheets. You can also save contacts straight to your phone's address book as a .vcf.

Does it work offline at the venue?

Scanning, notes and saving work without a connection. Cards are read by AI once you're back online, so poor venue Wi-Fi won't lose a lead.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Plugd doesn't run a cloud service for your contacts. If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it, so export anything you want to keep first.

Is Plugd on iPhone?

Plugd is available now on Android (7.0 or later) from Google Play. The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store.

What if a card is read wrong?

Tap the contact to fix any field, or choose Read again. Cards that couldn't be read are flagged under "to fix" on Home, and your photos and notes are always kept.
