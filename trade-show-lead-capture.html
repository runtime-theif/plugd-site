Plugd / Trade show lead capture

Free trade show lead capture app.

Plugd is a lead retrieval app for exhibitors that runs on your own Android phone. Scan every business card, badge QR code and NFC card at your booth, qualify the lead on the spot, and walk out with a CRM-ready list.

Free onGoogle Play Coming soon on theApp Store

Why exhibitors switch from rented scanners

Event lead retrieval devices are rented per show, tied to one organiser's badge system, and hand you a spreadsheet days later. Plugd works at any event, on the phones your team already carries, and every lead is in your hands the moment it's scanned.

  • Any event, any badge. Paper cards, QR codes on badges, digital business cards and NFC cards all land in one list.
  • Qualify at the booth. Tag each lead hot, warm or cold and flag who needs a follow-up.
  • Notes without typing. Say what you talked about while the card saves; the voice note is transcribed into the contact.
  • One list per event. Leads are filed under the show, counted, and duplicates are flagged.
  • Free, no account. Nothing to rent, no seats to buy, no sign-up for booth staff.

Scan conference badges and QR codes

Switch Plugd to QR and point it at the code on an attendee badge or a digital business card. Plugd reads vCard and MeCard codes straight into a contact, and keeps links so you can open the attendee's profile later.

Read NFC business cards

Switch to NFC and hold the card or tag to the back of your phone. Contact details written to the card are saved into the same event list as your paper cards. NFC needs an Android phone that supports it, with NFC turned on.

A booth workflow that takes seconds

  1. Before the show, create an event in Plugd and add your AI key once.
  2. At the booth, hold up the card or badge. Plugd captures it hands-free.
  3. Say a quick note: "Needs pricing for 50 seats, call Tuesday."
  4. Tap hot, warm or cold.
  5. After the show, filter to hot leads and export a CSV for HubSpot, Salesforce or Zoho, or an Excel file for the team.

Your leads stay yours

Plugd has no account and no cloud copy of your contacts. Leads are stored on your phone until you export them, and card photos go only to the AI provider you choose. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Is Plugd a replacement for event lead retrieval scanners?

For many exhibitors, yes. Plugd captures paper cards, badge QR codes and NFC cards on your own phone, at any event, for free. If a show only exposes attendee data through its own encrypted badge system, you may still need the organiser's device for those badges.

How many leads can I capture?

There is no scan limit. Plugd is free and leads are stored on your phone.

Can several booth staff use it?

Yes. Each person installs Plugd on their own phone and scans under the same event name, then exports. Combine the CSV files before importing them into your CRM.

Does it work with bad venue Wi-Fi?

Yes. Scanning, notes and tagging work offline. Cards are read by AI once you're back online.

Which CRMs does it work with?

Any CRM that imports CSV or Excel, including HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM and Pipedrive.
